How it works

Your goals. Your pace.
Zero excuses.

BFF tracks your movement, nutrition, and mindset - then holds you accountable the way only a best friend can.

Step 01

Pick your BFF

Choose a persona that matches your vibe - Bro, Bestie, Queen, or Ride or Die. Each coaches with a completely different style.

Step 02

Set your goals

Tell BFF what you're working toward. Weight, nutrition, fitness - it builds a real plan around your actual life and schedule.

Step 03

Track daily

Log meals, steps, mood, and weight. BFF pays attention across all four screens so you always know exactly where you stand.

Step 04

Stay accountable

BFF checks in daily, calls you out when you slip, and celebrates every win. No sugarcoating. No giving up on you. Just results.
